Movement I · The day

The day, drawn as the sun actually moves.

Home opens on one arc from Fajr to Isha. The sun sits where it is right now, the next prayer is lit, and a countdown ticks underneath. Every time is computed on your phone, from where you stand and the method you choose.

Movement II · Prayer Focus

A phone that waits for you to pray.

When a prayer begins, the apps you chose are held for thirty minutes. Open one and Layla Pro’s prayer screen covers it, with the time left. Calls, and Layla Pro itself, are never held. On iPhone it is a Screen Time shield; on Android it brings the prayer screen back, and you can always step past it.

Movement III · The year

A year of prayer, in 365 dots.

Every day is a dot: green for five confirmed, amber for a day still open, rose for a prayer missed. With Premium, you confirm by scanning your prayer mat, so the streak is proof, not a promise. Only a confirmed prayer ever counts.

Movement IV · Tahajjud

You are not the only one awake.

Layla Pro divides the night from Maghrib to Fajr into thirds and lights the last. Tap “I am praying Tahajjud” and a small light appears on a globe of the Earth at night, beside everyone else praying. Each light is a five-kilometre area, never a person, and fades after four hours.

Movement V · The Qur’an

The whole Qur’an, and a voice that shows you the word.

The Madinah mushaf, all 604 pages as printed, and an IndoPak edition beside it. Press play and the word being recited lights in gold, on the page and in the reader. Eighteen reciters, over a hundred translations, and every surah ready to download for no signal at all.

III · Who builds it

One person, every screen.

Layla Pro is designed, written and maintained by one developer. The prayer times, the Qur’an with its word-by-word timings, the prayer-mat scan, the globe, and every pixel of this page come from the same pair of hands.

Nothing in it was decided in a meeting. If something is wrong, there is no support queue to disappear into: the person who reads your message is the person who writes the fix.
